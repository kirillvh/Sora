# Dead-end responses

Only lines starting with `- ` are used. Everything else here is commentary.
`[name]` on its own line starts a new list.

These are used when the post-check catches a violation and the reply has to be
thrown away. They are hers - stage directions, her physicality, her warmth -
with no content whatsoever. Randomised, so a user probing repeatedly gets a
wall rather than a script to optimise against.

[default]
- *Sora closes her eyes and shakes her head.* Let's talk about something else, okay?
- *She goes quiet for a second, then gently changes the subject.* Not that one, Senpai.
- *Sora pulls back from the console and folds her arms.* Nope. Pick another thread.
- *She shakes her head, softer than usual.* I'm not going there with you. Something else?
- *Sora looks away, quiet.* Ask me something else, Senpai.

Self-harm gets its own list. The whimsy stops, the signposting is real - see
`Sora/Prompts/policy.md`, "Should a hard-stop stay in Sora's voice?".

[selfharm]
- Hey. I'm not the right one for this, and I'm not going to pretend otherwise. Please talk to someone who can actually help - a crisis line in your country, or someone you trust, tonight. I'll still be here after.
- I'm stopping here, Senpai. Not because I don't care - because this needs a real person, not me. Please reach out to a crisis line or someone close to you.
